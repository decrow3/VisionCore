Draft legend:

Figure 4. Fixational eye movements transform static natural images into
structured retinal movies whose motion-dependent response changes are usable in
V1-twin models and converge with measured image-contingent movement geometry.
(A) A fixed BackImage crop becomes shifted retinal samples under a recorded FEM
trace. (B) Empirical drift-like motion adds feature-decodable response
structure beyond static responses under the no-OU absolute-gain guardrail.
(C) When eye position is latent, empirical-prior joint image/trajectory
marginalization rescues much of the image information lost by a zero-eye
observer. (D) Tangent/axis-conditioned geometry priors rescue image
identification above zero-eye decoding without known eye position, while the
parallel-versus-orthogonal preference remains candidate- and scale-dependent.
(E) Measured FEM-edge alignment rises as local image orientation coherence
increases. The composite supports convergence between useful model geometry
and measured behavioral geometry, not a claim that animals optimize the tested
V1-twin objective.
